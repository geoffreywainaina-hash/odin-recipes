Lasagna

Home CHAPATI UGALI

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian baked pasta dish made with layers of flat pasta sheets, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese. It is baked until golden, with a soft, hearty interior and a deliciously cheesy top. Its combination of savory flavors, creamy textures, and aromatic herbs makes it a comforting and satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • 500 g minced beef
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2–3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cups tomato puree
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dried basil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 3 tbsp butter
  • 3 tbsp all-purpose flour
  • 3 cups milk
  • A pinch of salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • A pinch of nutmeg (optional)
