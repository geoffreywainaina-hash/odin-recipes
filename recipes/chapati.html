Chapati

Home LASAGNA UGALI

Description

Chapati is a soft, golden-brown flatbread with a beautifully layered texture and a lightly crisp exterior. Made from simple ingredients, it's a staple of East African cuisine, loved for its versatility and comforting taste. Whether paired with beans, beef stew, green grams or enjoyed on its own, chapati is one of those simple foods that never disappoints.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup warm water (adjust when neede)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil (for the dough)
  • ¼ cup cooking oil (for layering and frying)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)

Cooking Steps

  1. Prepare the dough
  2. Knead the dough
  3. Let the dough rest
  4. Divide the dough
  5. Roll out the dough
  6. Create the layers
  7. Roll again
  8. Cook the chapati
  9. Fry until golden
  10. Serve
