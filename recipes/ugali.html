Ugali

Home LASAGNA CHAPATI

Description

Ugali is a traditional East African staple made by cooking maize flour in boiling water and stirring it until it forms a firm, smooth, and dough-like consistency. It has a mild flavor, making it a versatile accompaniment to vegetables, meat stews, fish, and sauces. Ugali is widely enjoyed for its filling nature, simplicity, and cultural significance.

Ingredients
  • 2 cups maize flour (unga wa mahindi)
  • 4 cups water (adjust depending on desired consistency)
Cooking steps
  1. Boil the water
  2. Add maize flour
  3. Stir and thicken
  4. Knead the ugali
  5. Cook thoroughly
  6. Shape and serve
